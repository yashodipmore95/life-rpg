Delete your Life RPG account?

This permanently removes your account, profile, quest history, journal entries, achievements, coin redemptions, and feedback. There is no way to undo this or recover your data afterward.

Leave onboarding?

Your saved progress will be kept. You can continue where you left off the next time you sign in.

Reset Progress?

This will permanently remove this user's gameplay progress. Their account and preferences will remain active.

Permanently delete this user?

This permanently deletes the user's account and all associated LIFE RPG data. This action cannot be undone.
